## Behavior Analysis

**11** behaviors in total, each backed by evidence, and all are statically available.

### 1. Unpack the UPX layer

`T1027.002` · Confidence **High** · Static

The sample is a UPX 4.24-packed x86-64 ELF whose entry point lies in a self-decompressing stub at 0x15ec728, with overall entropy 7.943; static unpacking yields the 1,288,624-byte original program.

> **Evidence**: Unpacked-entry VA 0x15ec728; unpacked output sha256 8999bbc5649b6b2bfe6d11adfcf5041f86cada950393fdbd6ae7e879ef009a14
> 
> **Source**: `ref:40f1759ac3` · `ref:28b912556d` · `ref:e713e9cbb3`

## What Not to Misjudge

:::important
The following conclusions are **easy to misjudge**; keep them in mind when citing this report.

- Do not assert use of specific encryption algorithms such as AES or RSA from the enckey field alone; static analysis did not recover the algorithm constants or the key's purpose.
- No real C2 session or request/response was obtained in this run; any connect attempt or DNS failure does not mean a control channel was established, and the C2 must not be marked dynamically confirmed.
- Do not classify the sample as ransomware or a wiper: the available evidence points to botnet, download and mining capabilities, with no file-encryption or destructive behavior found.
- Do not assert a systemd unit or crontab was written on the victim host: static analysis shows only templates and install branches, and the dynamic installation result was not observed.
:::

**Limitations and pending verification**

1. Isolated dynamic execution returned no usable process, syscall or network events in this attempt, so there is no real request/response, packet capture or memory evidence.
2. The C2 address, ParentIp relationship and parameter format come from static strings and the file-tail JSON and have not been verified at runtime.
3. Only 128 bytes of base64 enckey data were extracted; the encryption algorithm, key derivation and actual purpose are unconfirmed.
4. The systemd unit name, final on-disk filename and cron file path could not be fully recovered from static data.
