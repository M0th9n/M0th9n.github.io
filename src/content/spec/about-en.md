# About

Hi, I'm **M0th9n** 🐹.

This is where I share my latest work in binary security — turning the resources and knowledge I already have into reusable, openly published reports and articles.

## What this site covers

- **Malware analysis**: an in-house agent produces static/dynamic analysis reports on recent, real samples
- **Vulnerability research**: malware first; reproduction and discovery of vulnerabilities will follow
- **Game cheats**: occasional posts on the internals of game cheats and how they're implemented (still learning)

## About the content

All analysis is performed in an isolated environment. The technical details in these articles are intended for defensive research, security education and incident-response reference only.

Mistakes happen. Discussion and corrections are welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/M0th9n) — that discussion is what keeps this going.
